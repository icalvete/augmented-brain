The weekly review

A week after your last review, you open Claude. It says: “The weekly review is due: 3 captures in the inbox and 1 task untouched for two weeks. Shall we do it? About ten minutes.” You say yes, and it goes through each item with you, one at a time.

The weekly review keeps the system honest. Without it, the inbox fills up and stalled tasks pile up in silence.

On this page

  • What it goes through
  • When it comes up
  • With Claude
  • In the panel
  • What is kept
  • Two computers

What it goes through

  • The inbox, with what other spaces sent here, marked with where it came from. Each capture becomes a task, goes to an area or to knowledge, or is dropped.
  • Tasks untouched for 14 days or more. Each one goes on as it is, goes to someday, or is closed.
  • The dates due in the coming week: watch dates of tasks and inbox reminders.

When it comes up

At the start of a session, when the last review is 7 days old or more (with no review yet, since the oldest capture or untouched task) and there is something to go through. Claude offers it once, in one line, when it does not interrupt other work. You can also ask for it any time: “let's do the weekly review”.

With Claude

Claude lists what there is, then asks about each item. You decide; it does the rest: opens the task, files the note, drops the capture. At the end it saves what was decided and takes the processed captures out of the inbox.

In the panel

The panel has a Weekly review page. Each capture has its buttons: a task, a reference, a lesson, or drop it. Each untouched task goes on, or goes to some day (or you open it to close it). Sending a capture to an area is not one of the buttons: ask Claude for that. What you decide is noted as you go, in a draft on this computer, so you can leave and come back. When you finish, it is all written in the review's record.

What is kept

Each review is a note, reviews/review-<date>.md, with what was decided. Processed captures leave the inbox. Nothing else is deleted.

Two computers

Captures added on two computers are both kept. One catch: a capture you processed on one computer can come back if the other computer captured something at the same time. It just shows up again at the next review.
