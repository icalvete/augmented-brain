Working from your phone or another computer

Your notes live on one computer. You can still work with them from your phone, a tablet or another computer. There are two ways, and they do different things:

  • Talk to Claude on that computer, from anywhere: remote access. You pick a session in claude.ai/code or Claude Desktop, and work as if you were sitting there.
  • Look at your notes in the panel, from anywhere: the panel from another device. Today, your tasks, the inbox, everything the panel shows.

You can use one, the other, or both. Remote access works on Linux, macOS and Windows; the panel from another device works wherever Tailscale does.

Set it up once

On the computer that holds your notes:

  1. Remote access. Run augmented-brain remote install, then do what it asks: let Claude trust the folder and, on Linux, turn on “linger” so it survives a reboot (on macOS and Windows it starts when you log in). Details in Remote access.
  2. The panel. Install Tailscale on the computer and on your phone, signed in to the same account, then run augmented-brain panel remote. It prints an address and a QR code. Details in The panel from another device.

augmented-brain doctor checks both: a line for the door of each space and, once you have used it, one for the panel from another device.

Which session to use

Remote access opens one door per space, named <computer name>-<space>, such as my-laptop-personal. A session started through it begins in that space's first work folder, so Claude loads that space's notes and rules.

  • In claude.ai or Claude Desktop, pick the environment with that name and start a session. You can start as many as you like.
  • To work on a task, say so: “resume task-bake-bread”. Claude reads the task and carries on where it was left.
  • To reach another space, use that space's door. Notes never cross between spaces.

When it stops answering

  • The computer is asleep. A laptop that sleeps drops the connection. It comes back when it wakes up.
  • The session was archived in claude.ai, or ended on Claude's side. On Linux it comes back by itself, in about ten minutes; on macOS and Windows it takes three steps by hand. Both are in Remote access.
  • Your Claude login expired. Run claude once on the computer and sign in again.

augmented-brain remote status says how each door stands, and what to do. Other problems are in Troubleshooting.

A session for each task

On Linux, the session behind a space's door can open another one for a task (“open me a session for the website task”). It shows up in your list within a minute, named after the task, in its folder, already reading it. Open as many as you have tasks. On macOS and Windows it is not available yet. See A session per task.

Keep it safe

Both are doors to your computer. Remote access is a permanent one: anyone who gets into your Claude account can work on the machine through it. Protect that account with a strong password and two-step verification, keep Claude asking before it acts (never bypassPermissions), and remove the doors you do not use: augmented-brain remote uninstall and augmented-brain panel stop. The panel's address works only for your own devices in your Tailscale network, and only while the panel runs.
